Notes & safety
Auto-poll runs only when the app is started with EMAIL_MONITOR_BACKGROUND=1; otherwise use "Check now". Parsing sale emails is best-effort — unmatched or unparsed sales are logged for review and never guess at a delist. The mailbox password is stored in inventory.db, so treat that file as a secret; a dedicated address or app password is strongly recommended.

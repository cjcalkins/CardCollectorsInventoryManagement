Card identification service

When the local reader can't confidently identify a card (below 60% — glossy/foil nameplates, numbers lost in foil or halftone noise), send the front image to an external service to read the name, number, set and rarity. It runs only on cards the local reader misses. This applies to both auto-import and the manual “Read Card & Find Matches” on a card's detail page.
